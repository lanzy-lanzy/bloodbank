Why it feels alive

◈

Real refraction

Vertex-displaced geometry with a Fresnel rim — the blob bends the scene behind the glass exactly where light would expect it to.

◎

Cursor lighting

A point light tracks your pointer across every card as a radial sheen, and pulls the 3D highlight with it. One rAF-throttled listener, zero layout thrash.

↕

Scroll depth

GSAP ScrollTrigger maps scroll velocity into shader distortion and layers parallax at three depths, so the page reads as a physical space instead of a flat sheet.
