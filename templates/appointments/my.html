Schedule & preparation guidance

You do not need to request or book an appointment yourself. The blood bank administrator sets required donation schedules and sends notifications to all registered donors.

Before your scheduled visit

  • Get adequate rest and stay well hydrated before arriving.
  • Eat a light, iron-rich meal prior to donation and avoid alcohol as instructed.
  • Bring a valid government-issued ID for verification.
  • Final eligibility is always determined by staff screening on the day.
