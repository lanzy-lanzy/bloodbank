{# ---------- How it works ---------- #}

The journey of a unit

From donation to transfusion

Every stage is an enforced state machine — a unit cannot skip screening, testing or verification, and clinical rules are configured and approved by our medical administrators, never assumed.

  1. STEP 01

    Register & screen

    Donors register, book appointments and complete questionnaire-based screening with eligibility checks.

  2. STEP 02

    Donate

    Collection is recorded and each donation becomes a quarantined, traceable blood bag.

  3. STEP 03

    Test & verify

    Required tests must be non-reactive and verified before any bag can enter available stock.

  4. STEP 04

    Match & release

    Hospitals request components; allocation follows the approved compatibility matrix with a full ledger trail.
