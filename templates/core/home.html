{# ---------- Who we serve ---------- #}

Built for everyone in the chain

One system, four connected perspectives. Clinical rules — compatibility, eligibility, shelf life — are configured and approved by our medical administrators, never assumed.

Donors

Book appointments, track screening results, see your donation history and the reward points every gift earns.

Hospitals & blood banks

Request components, track approval and allocation status, and receive released units with full traceability.

Safety & audit

Testing state machines, admin-approved compatibility rules and an append-only audit trail behind every action.

From donation to transfusion

  1. 1

    Donate

    Screened, recorded and turned into traceable bags at collection.

  2. 2

    Test

    Required tests verified by staff before any bag can go available.

  3. 3

    Request & match

    Hospitals request components; allocation uses the approved compatibility matrix.

  4. 4

    Release

    Double-checked release with a full ledger trail, right through to transfusion.
